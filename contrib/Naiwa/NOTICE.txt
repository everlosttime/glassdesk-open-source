GlassDesk builds the managed companion from the attributed source. The
animation and audio resources come from naiwa-pet-v2.5.1-portable.zip.
Runtime-only builds use resources imported by the user from that ZIP.
Builds with bundled resources record their sizes and SHA-256 hashes in
assets-manifest.json. The upstream executable, scripts and manuals are
not included in either build.

Upstream resource notice (README, Artwork/Assets): .nwp files are generated
from PNG/GIF/WebP frames, and audio is locally converted/processed. Where the
artwork involves third-party copyright, upstream asks users to confirm its
origin and authorization before commercial use. Personal learning and exchange
are unaffected according to the upstream notice. No additional artwork rights
are claimed by GlassDesk.
